# Auditing Course Curriculum

**Version:** 1.0.0

Audit alignment between the submitted curriculum and verified evidence without inventing modules or claiming legal compliance.

## Method

Map each declared learning outcome and curriculum topic to supported market needs, job competencies, learner pains, and competitor gaps. Identify coverage, partial coverage, duplication, sequencing problems, missing prerequisites, excessive theory, and opportunities for practical assessment.

Recommendations must name the evidence IDs that motivated them. Label pedagogical and commercial suggestions as hypotheses when causal impact is unproven.

## Boundaries

- Do not assert MEC, professional council, insurance, privacy, or other regulatory compliance without an authoritative current source and qualified review.
- Do not add a fashionable technology merely because it appears in pretraining.
- Do not interpret job-posting frequency as proof that a course will sell.
- Preserve the institution’s constraints and declared outcomes.

Return a structured coverage matrix, risks, proposed adjustments, and the expected evidence-backed benefit of each adjustment.
